Your team makes AI media in separate tools.

Nitrate keeps the brief, files, prompts, feedback, and finished outputs together in one project.

The problem: you lose track of who made what, with which prompt, and what needs review.
Get plugin access See the plugin flow

The handoff breaks after the brief is sent.

The lead sends a brief. Each creator works in their own AI tool. The work comes back as files, links, screenshots, and prompt notes scattered across chats.

Now the leader has to ask: who made this, what prompt produced it, what assets were used, and is this ready for client review?

Nitrate fixes that handoff. The brief goes out as a packet. The work comes back attached to that packet.

Nitrate gives each project one packet.

The packet tells every creator what to make, what files to use, where to put outputs, and what to send back.

Brief

The project goal, direction, constraints, and review criteria.

Files

The input assets and references every creator should use.

People

Who is assigned, which tool they are using, and what they are exploring.

Returns

The output, prompt, notes, tool, status, and feedback.
