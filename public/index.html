The project leader’s problem is not generation. It is control.

Your creators already use Claude, Claude Code, Runway, Higgsfield, Fal, Replicate, and their own local clankers. The issue is what happens around the generation.

Briefs become Slack threads. Inputs live in scattered folders. Prompts stay inside private chats. Returns arrive as links, downloads, screenshots, and mystery filenames.

Nitrate turns the brief into an operating packet. The leader dictates how the work should be done, the clanker handles the packet mechanics, and every return comes back with context.

A packet is the unit of command.

Not a moodboard. Not a shared folder. A packet is the exact instruction object your AI media team works from.

What to make

Campaign brief, creative direction, constraints, banned looks, and review criteria.

What to use

Input assets, references, brand material, previous returns, and tool-specific guidance.

Who does what

Creator assignments mapped to the clanker or agent workspace they already use.

How it returns

Required folders, media files, prompt notes, tool/model context, and handoff material.
