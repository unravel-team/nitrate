Announcement

nitrate opens its design-partner preview for AI video agencies

September 4, 2026: nitrate is opening a private preview for agencies whose creators produce work in separate AI workspaces. A lead packages the campaign brief, brand assets, assignments, required output folders, and review criteria once. Nitrate delivers that project to each creator's AI coding agent or Higgsfield Supercomputer workspace, then brings every finished file and its creation context back to the lead.

"AI video creation became individual. nitrate makes it collaborative again: one campaign brief goes out, every creator's work comes back with context."
- nitrate founding team

Why now

AI creation moved to individuals. Agency production is still a team sport.

The work sits in personal agent sessions, Higgsfield projects, and download folders. Leads repeatedly explain the same campaign, creators miss brand inputs or handoff requirements, and returned videos arrive without the prompt, assignment, or review context needed to make a decision.

What ships in the MVP

Lead packages the campaign

Write the brief once, attach real brand assets, assign creative routes, and set the required return folders.

Nitrate delivers it

Creators pull the complete assignment in Codex or Claude Code. Higgsfield Supercomputer can receive it through Nitrate's remote MCP.

Creators return the result

The finished media comes back with its creator, assignment, prompt, tool, notes, and source handoff intact.

The lead decides

Approve, request changes, reject, or reopen the exact returned file from one review queue.

About nitrate

nitrate is the collaboration layer and working DAM for an AI video agency's agents. A lead packages the campaign once; creators receive the same brief and source files inside Codex, Higgsfield Supercomputer, or Claude Code; and every result returns with enough context to review. nitrate is onboarding design partners from AI video agencies and AI-native creative studios.

Facts and forward-looking claims.

Customer logos, funding, pricing, SLAs, and compliance certifications are not announced. Codex and Claude Code support is provided through Nitrate's plugins. Higgsfield Supercomputer connectivity uses the open MCP interface and is not a claim of a native partnership. C2PA inspection/export and regulatory reporting remain roadmap objectives.
