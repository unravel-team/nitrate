Why now

AI creation moved to individuals. Media production is still a team sport.

Media teams are using powerful tools, but the work sits in personal chats, local folders, and individual AI setups. Leads cannot reliably push the same brief to everyone, enforce the return structure, or understand which creator returned what.

What ships in the MVP

Brief packets

Leads define the brief, input assets, references, constraints, and expected output folders.

Plugin login

Creators authenticate from the clanker plugin and receive structured work packets instead of loose notes.

Returned work

Files come back attached to creator, prompt notes, assignment, and review status.

Lead review

Approve, reject, request changes, compare returns, and share selected work.

About nitrate

nitrate is a clanker plugin and collaboration layer for AI media teams. Creators log in from the plugin to receive team packets inside their AI workspace, then sync returned media, prompts, notes, and handoff files back into one reviewable project. nitrate is onboarding design partners from AI-native studios, advertising teams, music-video production, and enterprise brand-content groups.

Facts and forward-looking claims.

Customer logos, funding, pricing, SLAs, direct integrations, and compliance certifications are not announced. Direct clanker sync, C2PA inspection/export, and EU AI Act-oriented reporting are roadmap objectives, not current certifications.
